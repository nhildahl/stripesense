What it does

Simple to use. Smart underneath.

Open it and your next numbers are right there. After the run, snap the slip, tap Won or Lost, and save. Every run you log teaches the AI more about your car.

AI-driven predictions

Throttle stop or dial-in and the delay box for each lane, big on the first screen. The AI learns your car's own weather, 60-foot and stop-time effects from your log, and a Smart Blend trusts each of five formulas by how well it has called your runs.

AI slip reading

Take a picture of your time slip and an AI text reader on your own device fills in both lanes, with no signal. Slips differ from track to track, so numbers it isn't sure of are highlighted. Check before you save.

Pager

No signal? Use the pager.

Your numbers go from the laptop straight to a pager in your pocket or on the dash, by long-range digital radio.

  • No license, no internet, no cell service. It uses the license-free 915 MHz band in the US, up to about a mile in the open.
  • Choose what each line shows, like the send time and which way your stop is trending (next update).
  • Just yours: a private, encrypted channel only your radios can read.

FAQ

What does "AI-driven" mean here?

Two things, both running on your own laptop with no account, key or internet: a learning model that fits your car's own weather, 60-foot and stop-time effects from your runs and re-weights its formulas after every run, and a neural-network text reader for your slips. It isn't a chatbot, and your runs aren't sent anywhere to be analyzed. Predictions are still estimates, and they get better as you log more runs.

How much does it cost?

The 30-day trial is free, with every feature on. Pricing is being finalized. After the trial, predictions, the Lanes numbers and sending need a license key; your log and data always stay yours.

I race by myself. Does it still work?

That's who it's made for. Log your run when you get back to the trailer, and Stripe Sense can send the numbers for the next round to your phone, or to a pager, by itself whenever the weather changes them.

What if there's no signal at the track?

The predictions, slip reading and the field run on your laptop with no internet. Phone messages (through the free ntfy service) and online weather need a connection, like track Wi-Fi or a hotspot. For no signal at all, use the pager: two small radios on a private channel. The pager kit is coming soon. It works with the Windows version, over USB. Android support is built but not tested yet.

Can it read a time slip, or a ladder?

A photo of your time slip fills in both lanes. Reading a photo of a posted ladder is coming in the next update and is still being tested; qualifying sheets can be typed or pasted, and reading them from a photo hasn't been tested yet. Everything is read on your own device, with no signal. Slips and sheets differ from track to track and photos aren't perfect: numbers it isn't sure of are highlighted, but a few mistakes can slip through, so check every row before you save. Lay the sheet flat, fill the picture and keep it sharp.

Where is my data kept?

Your runs, cars and photos stay on your own computer. Only what you choose to send to your phone leaves it, through the free ntfy service. You can make a backup file at any time and move it to a new laptop.
